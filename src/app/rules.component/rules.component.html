Blackjack Rules

  • 🃎 Each player starts with two cards.
  • 🎯 The goal is to get as close to 21 as possible without going over.
  • 👤 The dealer draws cards until reaching at least 17.
  • 🔥 Blackjack is achieved with an Ace and a 10-value card.
  • 💥 If a player goes over 21, it’s an automatic loss (a "bust").
  • 💰 Before each round, players place their bet using the available chips.
  • 🔄 Bets must be placed during the betting phase; once confirmed, they are locked until the end of the round.
  • 🎲 Winnings are calculated based on the bet: win = 2×bet, blackjack = 2.5×bet, loss = 0.
